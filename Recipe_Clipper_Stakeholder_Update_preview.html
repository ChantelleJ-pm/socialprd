What improved

  • 126 supported sites were added on Feb 9
  • User error rate improved from 35% to 25% of submissions in the immediate pre/post Feb 9 window
  • Save-click-to-success conversion improved from 47% to 54% after the site expansion

Stakeholder takeaway

  • We are making progress on the leading KPIs we can validate in this export: reliability, speed, and external recipe adoption.
  • Collections and repeat sessions are still important, but they are not measurable from this export.
