## Stakeholder takeaway

- This version is best if you want the KPI slide to reflect performance **from the beginning of launch**
- It is stronger for telling a cumulative launch story than the post-mid-Feb snapshot
- The main current gap is still **user-based outcome metrics**, especially:
  - successful saves per active user
  - % of active users saving
  - repeat usage / retention

## Footnote
Launch-to-date counts come from the exported Total row. Time-based averages and share metrics use the exported Average definitions unless analytics confirms a different preferred method.
